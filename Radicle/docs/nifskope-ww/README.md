## How to use them

- **Reference, not authority.** Nothing here has been checked by this repository, including the gate results the notes report. Check a fact against BGS, vanilla files or our own reverse engineering before code relies on it.
- **Local paths are the fork author's.** `scratchpad/...` and `E:\...` paths point at that author's machine, and those tools, scripts and reports are not in this repository.
- **Names in the notes are the fork's.** "We" and "our" mean the fork, and lane names such as HKX1 are its own work items.
